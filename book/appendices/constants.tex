Useful photon conversions are
\begin{align}
  E &= h\nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda},\\
  E[\si{\electronvolt}] &\simeq \frac{12398.42}{\lambda[\angstrom]},\\
  \lambda_{\rm obs} &= (1+z)\lambda_{\rm rest}.
\end{align}
